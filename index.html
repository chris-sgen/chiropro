Your first visit

Forty-five minutes.
No mystery, no pressure.

The single most common thing we hear afterwards is that nobody had ever actually explained what was wrong before. So that is what the first visit is for.

01

Talk it through

What hurts, when it started, what makes it worse, what you have already tried, and what you need to get back to.

about 15 min
02

Physical assessment

Movement and range testing, plus orthopaedic and neurological tests. Imaging only if the findings genuinely call for it.

about 15 min
03

See what we found

We show you the assessment on screen and explain it in plain English. You are welcome to record it or bring someone.

about 10 min
04

A plan, with a number

How many visits we expect, what it will cost, and the two or three things you do at home between them. In writing.

about 5 min

For the things we treat most — lower-back pain, neck pain and cervicogenic headache — spinal manipulation and mobilisation are supported by clinical guidelines as part of a package alongside exercise and advice. We do not claim it treats conditions unrelated to the musculoskeletal system, and we will tell you when the evidence is thin.
