How it started

The clinic began because of a bad first appointment.

Dr Amara Whitfield spent eleven minutes with a chiropractor in 2006, left with a twenty-four-visit plan she had not agreed to, and no explanation of what was actually wrong with her back. She paid for four, stopped going, and the pain resolved on its own about the time the plan would have ended.

ChiroPro opened on Mill Row three years later with one bench, one practitioner and one operating rule: nobody leaves without knowing what we found and how many visits we think it will take. It is written into every plan, and it is the number we get held to.

The Northgate clinic opened in 2016 and Harbourside in 2021, both because patients kept driving further than they should have to. We have deliberately not opened a fourth — three is the number where all of us still know every patient on the books.
