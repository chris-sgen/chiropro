§4

Voice

Descriptors shared across bundles; taglines tuned to C

Sounds like

  • We fix pain fast
  • No long treatment plans. No unnecessary procedures.
  • Care within days, not months
  • Getting care shouldn’t feel complicated
  • New patient appointment includes: focused spinal exam

Never sounds like

  • Unlock your body’s innate healing potential
  • Realign your energy and restore balance
  • Subluxation-free living starts here
  • The miracle cure your doctor won’t tell you about
plain-spokenevidence-ledcalmunhurriedspecificwarm-professionalnon-alarmist

Sentence pattern: a short declarative claim, then one longer sentence that qualifies it with a number, a timeframe or a named technique. Specificity register is very-specific-named-thing — name the condition, the assessment, the visit count, the price. Never gesture at wellness in the abstract.
