Since 2009 · the original

Riverside

The first clinic, on the ground floor of a converted mill building with tall windows onto Mill Row. Three treatment rooms and the largest of the three waiting areas.

Getting here: free parking in the Mill Row lot behind the building, two minutes on foot. The 14 and 22 buses stop on Riverside Avenue, four minutes away.

Access: step-free from the street and from the car park. Accessible WC. One treatment room has a height-adjustable bench — tell us at booking if you need it.

Since 2016 · latest evening hours

Northgate

On the square, above the pharmacy. This is the clinic that stays open to eight, which makes it the one most people book when they cannot take time off work.

Getting here: Aldergate Square multi-storey is directly opposite and the first ninety minutes are free. Northgate station is a six-minute walk.

Access: lift to the first floor from the square-level entrance, step-free throughout. Accessible WC on the same floor.

Since 2021 · movement studio

Harbourside

The newest clinic and the only one with a separate movement studio, which is where the rehabilitation and pregnancy work happens. Opens at seven for people heading to work afterwards.

Getting here: on-street parking on Quay Lane is free before nine and after six; the Harbour car park is a four-minute walk. The 8 bus stops at the end of the lane.

Access: step-free from Quay Lane. Accessible WC. Pregnancy tables in two rooms, plus the studio for postnatal sessions.
