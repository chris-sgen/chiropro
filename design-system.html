§1

Tokens

design-system/tokens.css · flat --ds-* per BRAND-TOKEN-SCHEMA

The full :root block. Values trace to verticals/chiropro.md §4.bundles.C. No page in this site may use a literal hex — every surface reads a token, which is what lets one structural stylesheet skin the whole brand.

§8

Section rhythm reference

Rules every page must follow

The homepage band order. Inner pages inherit these rules: at least 2 non-light anchors, at least 1 in the middle band, never 4 consecutive sections sharing a background, at least 50% carrying imagery, and never 4 consecutive hairline-card sections. Verify with node design-system/verify-rhythm-local.mjs <page>.
